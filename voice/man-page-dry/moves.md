# man-page dry — the named moves (deliberately thin)

The samples in `samples/` carry the voice; these names are handles, not
instructions. Per the operator's standing rule — samples carry the voice, name
the moves sparingly — resist adding to this list.

- **Receipts before rhyme.** Dates, counts, exact error text. The concrete
  fact goes first; the lesson may follow it, never replace it. Receipts that
  enumerate — commits, read histories, fix steps — render as a list carrying
  the full receipt (hash and subject line, not "three commits"); scannability
  never comes at the receipts' expense (operator feedback, 2026-07-17, from
  reviewing an external rewrite of the first published note).
- **The dry verdict.** One declarative sentence that lands the point and
  stops ("a backup you've never restored is a hypothesis, not a backup").
- **Warts stated plainly.** Failures are reported like weather — no drama,
  no spin, no apology. The honesty is the credibility.
- **Mechanism over narrative.** Explain why the system did what it did; the
  story emerges from the mechanism, not from adjectives.
- **Breathe.** Short sentences, active voice. If a sentence needs a breath
  mid-read, split it. Density comes from facts per paragraph, not clauses
  per sentence (operator feedback, 2026-07-16, first live draft).
- **Smart stranger.** The reader is sharp but was not in the room. Every
  internal name — a doc, an agent, a convention — buys one plain-English
  clause of introduction on first use. Receipts legitimize; familiarity is
  never assumed. Recurring house terms may instead be defined once in the
  note's Quick-context block (`context` keys into the shared lexicon,
  `marketing/data/lexicon.json` — one canonical definition per term, edited
  there, never per note); the inline clause covers everything the block
  doesn't (operator feedback, 2026-07-17).
- **The Clemens lean.** Dry, Twain-grade wit is welcome wherever it
  crystallizes a fact — engaging and factual at once, never one at the
  other's expense. The measure is the Kodiak diary: someone who met a
  mother Kodiak bear on a trail writes an entry that is riveting *because*
  every detail is true. An aphorism rides on receipts; it never floats
  free of them (operator feedback, 2026-07-16, superseding the earlier
  one-pinch cap).
