# doorway-warm — the register for hooks, excerpts and per-platform titles

> **What this bottle covers.** The copy that has to earn a stranger's attention
> before any of our own furniture is around it: syndication headlines, LinkedIn
> excerpts, X posts, dev.to titles and tags, and the one-line teasers that point
> at a field note or a chapter.
>
> **What it does not cover.** Bodies. `_host/SEO.md` §Titles is explicit that
> per-surface *title* divergence is expected while the no-platform-voice-rewrites
> rule still governs the body — a dev.to post carries the apex body in its own
> register with a different headline on top. Never restyle a body into this
> voice.
>
> **Why it exists (2026-08-15).** We had one bottle, `man-page-dry`, and it was
> being asked to do this job too. It can't. Operator diagnosis, verbatim, because
> it names the failure better than any rule could: *"a little gruff, a little too
> grumpy dude saying 'I'll explain it to you if you're tough enough and I don't
> think you are.'"*
>
> That is not a flaw in man-page-dry. On the apex it is correct: the reader has
> already chosen to be there, and dryness reads as respect for their time. The
> dry verdict is **earned** by the receipts above it. Lift that same sentence
> into a feed, strip the 900 words that paid for it, and it becomes a stranger
> stating a conclusion you have no reason to accept — and a closed door.
>
> **The register in one line:** man-page-dry assumes the reader has committed;
> doorway-warm has to be worth committing to.

---

## The moves

- **The stake before the finding.** Dry delivers the verdict first because the
  page has already earned it. Here nothing has been earned yet, so lead with
  what it cost, what it nearly cost, or what was quietly wrong for five days.
  The finding lands *after* the reader has a reason to want it.

- **One idea. Not three.** A field note is three beats; a hook is one. Pick the
  single most surprising true thing and let the link carry the rest. A hook that
  summarizes a whole post gives the reader no reason to open it.

- **The number travels; the conclusion doesn't.** *"It reported running out of
  budget after spending 2% of it"* moves between people. *"Agent self-reports
  are unreliable"* does not — it is the lesson, and lessons are what the post is
  for. Lead with the fact that made you look twice.

- **The reader is never on trial.** No implied entry exam. If a term needs the
  post to parse, either spend a clause explaining it or pick a different hook.
  This is the specific corrective to the gruffness above: difficulty that the
  reader hasn't opted into reads as gatekeeping, not rigor.

- **Leave the door open at the end.** Dry closes with a verdict, which is the
  right ending for a page and the wrong one for a surface whose currency is
  replies. End on the link, the open question, or the thing you still don't
  know. A verdict invites nothing.

- **Warm is not soft, and it is definitely not LinkedIn.** The failure mode on
  the other side is manufactured feeling: *"I was shocked when…"*, the
  one-sentence-per-line cadence, the humblebrag setup, the engagement question
  nobody wants to answer. Prohibited outright. Warmth here means *welcoming*,
  not *performing* — the difference between holding a door and staging a
  reaction.

- **Receipts still rule.** Inherited unchanged from `man-page-dry`, and the one
  thing both bottles share: dates, counts, exact error text. A hook may be
  inviting; it may not be vague. If you cannot name the number, you do not have
  the hook yet.

- **Two links home, always.** Not a voice rule but it lives here because it dies
  everywhere else: `_host/SEO.md` §Syndication requires canonical plus at least
  two links back to `uzelhub.com` in any syndicated body. Non-negotiable, and
  the only structured authority flow the apex has.

## Deliberately no sample yet

`man-page-dry` carries a scar worth not repeating: its SHAPE order said three
beats, one screen, and the bottled sample was a four-section, 397-word example
that never got re-cut. For three weeks the Writer was told to be brief and shown
the opposite, and **every draft inherited the example rather than the rule**
(moves.md, 2026-08-08).

So this bottle opens empty on purpose. The operator is hand-cutting the first
exemplars to ground the style before any generator is pointed at it. A wrong
sample here would be worse than none, because samples out-argue doctrine.

Fill `samples/` only with copy that shipped and worked.
