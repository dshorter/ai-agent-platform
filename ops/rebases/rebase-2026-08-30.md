# Re-base — 2026-08-30

The ops calendar reached today with **42 of 44 todos overdue**, the oldest by 47
days. A list where nearly everything is late carries no signal: nothing is
prioritised because everything is equally overdue, and the dates have stopped
meaning anything a reader can act on.

This re-base makes the dates mean intent again. Applied plan:
[`rebase-2026-08-30.txt`](rebase-2026-08-30.txt).

## Why it got this bad — a missing verb, not neglect

Until today **nothing could change a due date**. `calendar-add` only appends;
`calendar-mark` only did lifecycle (cancel/complete) and explicitly never
touched dates. So moving one meant cancel-and-recreate — which re-keys the UID
and drops `RELATED-TO` links — or an operator editing the `.ics` by hand.

Moving a date was harder than ignoring it, so the dates rotted. That is a tooling
gap presenting as a discipline problem.

`calendar-mark --action defer` now exists (spec: [CALENDAR.md](../CALENDAR.md)),
with `--plan` for a reviewed batch. Every defer keeps a receipt in-band:

| Property | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `X-ORIGINAL-DUE` | the first due date the item ever had; written once, never rewritten |
| `X-DEFER-COUNT` | how many times it has been pushed |
| `X-DEFER-REASON` | why, on the most recent move |

That matters because a reschedule verb can make a rotten list look healthy. The
lateness is not erased, it is **promoted**: "due 09-23, deferred 1x, originally
07-14" is better signal than a date seven weeks stale, and an item that reaches
a defer count of 3 or 4 is one to kill rather than move again.

### 2 and 3. The Scout A/B pair — recommend cancelling

`scout-ab-flip-to-sonnet-r2` (due 07-19) and `scout-ab-week3-readout-r2` (07-26).

The flip **never happened** — `SCOUT_SYNTHESIS_MODEL` is not in `.env` at all — so

**What cancelling unlocks.** Both `define-why-now-in-scout-schema` (already
decided; only timing-gated on the readout) and `resume-release-drip-post-sonnet`

finished drafts queued behind it — collapses to its one real remaining gate,
`identity-profiles-setup`. Both are provisionally dated Sep 8 on that assumption.

If the model question is still live, it should be re-asked as a fresh experiment
against current ore, not resumed as this one.

## Moving a date from here

```
ops/calendar-mark --author operator --action defer \
    --uid <uid> --due YYYYMMDD --reason "why"
```

Or edit a plan file and `--plan` it (operator only, all-or-nothing, `--dry-run`
first). The receipt is automatic either way.
